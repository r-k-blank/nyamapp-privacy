Or request it inside the app

  1. Sign in to Nyam!.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm the request. Check the scheduled deletion shown by the app.
72-hour grace period: You can explicitly cancel the request during this period in the app or through support. Signing in alone does not cancel deletion. After the grace period, permanent processing removes the authentication account, associated application records and owned uploads once the server's dependency checks pass.

What can delay or outlive deletion?

Shared-household ownership, another user's references to your content or a service failure can require support intervention. The grace-period deadline is the start of eligibility, not a promise that all processing finishes at that moment. Contact support if a request remains pending.

Limited security, moderation, deletion-job and operational records or backups can remain for service protection, recovery or applicable legal obligations. Contact support for the treatment of your request, including older nutrition data. Shared data belonging to another member is not deleted merely because you requested deletion. Copies of exports or public content that you or another person saved outside the service are outside our control.

You may also email a request to remove particular information without deleting your entire account. Explain which information you mean; support will identify any functional or shared-data limits before acting.
