Delete your Nyam! account and data

Nyam! is operated by Rusu Catalin. You can request account deletion even if you no longer have the app installed.

Fastest option: inside the app

  1. Sign in to Nyam!.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm the request.

Request deletion by email

Send the request from the same email address used for your Nyam! account. Use the subject “Delete my Nyam account”. We may ask you to confirm control of the account before deletion.

Email the deletion request

What happens next: The account enters a 72-hour grace period. During that period you may cancel deletion by contacting support. After the grace period, the account and associated application records are scheduled for permanent deletion. Limited security or legal records may be retained where required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, or service integrity.
