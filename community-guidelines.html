Nyam! Community Guidelines

Effective: October 8, 2026 · Version 2026-10-08

Nyam! lets people publish recipes, reviews, text and photographs. By publishing, you agree to these rules.

Share safe, useful cooking content

  • Write clear ingredients, quantities and preparation steps.
  • Do not present medical claims or intentionally unsafe food-handling instructions.
  • Disclose important allergens where you know they are present. Users must still verify product labels themselves.
  • Do not post spam, scams, misleading information or unrelated promotion.

Respect people

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual exploitation or graphic violence.
  • Do not publish another person's private information.
  • Do not impersonate another person or organization.

Only publish content you may use

  • Use your own recipe text and photographs, or material you are legally allowed to publish.
  • Do not copy recipes, photographs, logos or other protected material without permission.
  • A recipe photograph must show the actual dish described by the recipe.

Moderation

Nyam! may review, reject, hide or remove content and may restrict accounts that repeatedly or seriously violate these rules. Users can report content and block other users. Reports are reviewed confidentially. If you believe a decision was incorrect, contact nyamapp.contact@gmail.com.

Emergency and medical notice

Nyam! is not an emergency or medical service. Contact local emergency services for urgent danger and a qualified healthcare professional for medical or dietary advice.
