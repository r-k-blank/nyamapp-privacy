Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Nyam! ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and password (encrypted) when you create an account.
  • Inventory data: Ingredients you add to your virtual fridge, including names, quantities, and expiry dates.
  • Preferences: Dietary preferences, allergies, cooking skill level, language setting, and notification preferences.
  • Recipe interactions: Favorites, reviews, notes, and cooking session history.
  • Shopping lists: Items you add to your shopping list.
  • Photos: Images you upload for your profile or recipes, stored securely.
  • Crash reports: Anonymous error logs and device information to help us fix bugs (via Sentry).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalize recipe recommendations based on your ingredients.
  • To save your inventory, preferences, and cooking history across devices.
  • To improve app stability through anonymous crash reporting.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers with Row Level Security enabled, ensuring only you can access your own data. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Backend and authentication (privacy policy).
  • Sentry: Crash reporting and error tracking (privacy policy).
  • Google Fonts: Typography loaded at runtime (privacy policy).

5. Your Rights

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's Profile screen.
  • You can request a copy of your data by contacting us.
  • You can opt out of crash reporting by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

Nyam! is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the date above.
