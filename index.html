Nyam! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · Culinary version

Developer/operator: r.k.blank. Nyam! helps you organize ingredients, find recipes and manage shopping and cooking. This policy explains the culinary version, the information it uses and your choices. Contact: nyamapp.contact@gmail.com.

1. Account and cooking information

  • Account: email address, account identifier, display name, avatar, sign-in and security information. Supabase Auth handles passwords; Nyam! does not store readable passwords.
  • Your kitchen: ingredients, optional quantities and expiry dates, product names, barcodes, brands, package information, import source and shopping lists. Restocking rules store the products you choose, minimum and target quantities, units and approved shopping contributions. Personal rules are private; rules created in a shared kitchen are accessible to its members. Confirmed receipt imports can retain the product label and a merchant identifier. We do not intentionally save a full receipt image or its complete text to your account.
  • Cooking and content: favorite and personal recipes, recipe notes and reviews, tools, cooking progress, cooking history, leftovers, culinary plans, household membership and shared contributions.
  • Settings and choices: cooking level, app language, notification settings, analytics choice, community-terms acceptance and acknowledgment of the culinary transition.
  • Uploads: images you choose to upload for your profile, recipes or tools. Public text and images can be reviewed for moderation. Avoid uploading private information about yourself or someone else.

We use this information to provide and synchronize the app, match recipes with your ingredients and tools, save your cooking progress, operate shared features, prevent abuse and handle support and deletion requests. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising. The culinary version has no advertising or payment SDK.

2. Camera, receipt and product lookup

  • Receipt photographs: when you choose a photo or camera image, Google ML Kit extracts Latin-script text on your device. Nyam! does not send that receipt photograph or the recognized full text to a cloud OCR service. Suggested products are saved to your account only after you confirm them.
  • Barcode lookup: scanning recognizes a code on your device. Choosing product lookup sends the product barcode to Open Food Facts. That service also receives ordinary network information such as your IP address. Nyam! does not attach your email, account identifier or complete inventory to this request. Product coverage and accuracy vary.
  • Moldovan fiscal QR: choosing this import requests the receipt from the official verification services at mev.sfs.md, mev-pre.sfs.md or freceipt.eservicii.md. Older sift-mev.sfs.md links are converted to the verification format. The request contains the receipt identifier or the register, amount, receipt number and date encoded in that QR, plus ordinary network information. This feature applies to supported Moldovan receipts.
  • Google ML Kit metrics: although image and text recognition runs locally, its SDKs can contact Google for model and compatibility updates and send SDK usage and diagnostics. These can include device model and operating-system information, app version, per-installation identifiers, latency, errors, feature events and input/output sizes. This is separate from Nyam!'s optional product analytics. See ML Kit privacy information and Android data disclosures.
  • Recipe link imports: when you request an import, the selected HTTPS URL is sent to a Supabase server function that retrieves a public recipe page from a supported site. The site operator receives that requested URL and the server's ordinary network information. Nyam! does not attach your account identifier, authorization token, inventory or shopping list to that outbound request, and it does not read your browser history or cookies. The downloaded page is returned for on-device structured recipe extraction; images and nutrition metadata are not imported. Review the result before saving. A saved imported recipe is private and retains its source URL, ingredients and steps. Operational quota counters retain your account ID, timestamps and attempt counts, without URLs or recipe text.

The culinary version does not offer voice entry or request microphone permission. Manual ingredient entry is available without camera permission.

3. Notifications, diagnostics and optional analytics

Expiry reminders are scheduled on your device when enabled. The app requests notification permission where the operating system requires it. You may disable reminders in app or system settings. We do not send ingredient names to a separate push-notification provider for these local reminders.

Nyam! uses Sentry error reporting only in builds configured with a Sentry endpoint. Diagnostic information can include app and platform versions, error categories and technical context. Account identity, secrets and free-form user content are not intended for diagnostic events. Screenshots and default personally identifiable information are disabled. Network services can still receive your IP address. See Sentry's privacy policy.

Nyam! product analytics is off by default. If you enable it in Profile → Settings, selected feature actions, including use of restocking and recipe link imports, and one daily app-activity mark contribute to daily totals grouped by app language and platform. To measure whether people return, the app also reports at most three retention milestones (first week, days 7–13 and days 28–34 after account creation); the service counts them per account-creation week. The request is authenticated so the service can verify consent; aggregate counter rows do not contain your account ID, email, ingredient names, URLs or free text. The app keeps on your device which day and milestones it already reported for your account; account deletion removes this marker. Turning this choice off stops subsequent contributions. Previously aggregated totals cannot be separated back into one person's history. This setting does not control ML Kit's SDK diagnostics or a configured Sentry endpoint.

4. Storage, sharing and public content

Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage and server functions. Access controls restrict private records. Supabase and other network providers process connection and operational information needed to deliver and protect their services. Providers may process data outside your country under their applicable terms and safeguards.

The app keeps account-scoped offline copies and cooking progress on your device. Signing out clears the app's private cached data for that account. Android application backup is disabled. A ZIP export you save through Android's document picker is a separate file under your control; signing out or deleting your account cannot remove copies you saved or shared yourself.

Restocking rules have a separate JSON export in the Restock essentials screen and are not yet included in the account ZIP. Imported recipes remain available for manual editing, cooking and export. The validation beta does not create a paid subscription. Copies of rule exports that you copy or save outside the app remain under your control.

When you join a Household, its members can see and change shared inventory, shopping information and shared cooking or leftover records. Display names and approved avatars may be shown to members. Sharing does not grant access to another member's email, private notes or private account data. Invitation codes expire after seven days. Leaving a Household removes your access; shared contributions can remain with the household.

Public recipes, reviews, display names and approved avatars may be visible to other users. Some approved image URLs may be accessible to anyone who has the link. Do not treat a profile or recipe image as confidential. Users may report content and block other users. Administrators review pending content and reports under the Community Guidelines.

5. Retired nutrition features and older records

The culinary version does not ask you to provide weight, height, date of birth, sex, fitness goals, medical conditions, allergies or dietary restrictions. Its profile and recipe-selection interfaces do not use those fields to provide health or nutrition recommendations. Nyam! is not a medical service and does not provide personalized medical, calorie or weight-management advice.

Earlier versions could hold physical-profile, dietary, allergy or nutrition information and nutrition-based plans. Removing a feature does not by itself erase older server records, internal history or backups. Such records may remain until the corresponding migration or deletion is completed; they are not used by the culinary profile. The culinary ZIP deliberately excludes retired Health fields and internal snapshots. Contact us if you need access to or deletion of older information rather than assuming it is included in that ZIP.

6. Retention and account deletion

We retain account content while your account remains active so the app can provide its features. You can edit or remove supported individual items in the app. Support messages are handled separately from app content; do not email passwords, payment details or identity documents unless an appropriate secure verification process has first been explained.

Deleting an account starts a 72-hour grace period. Signing in alone does not cancel it. You may explicitly cancel during the grace period in the app or contact support. After this period, the account enters the permanent-deletion process for its application records, owned uploads and authentication account.

Permanent deletion is not guaranteed to finish at the exact end of 72 hours. Shared households, another person's references to your content, ownership conflicts or service failures can require support intervention before processing continues. We do not mark a partially processed request as complete. Contact nyamapp.contact@gmail.com if your request remains pending.

Limited security, moderation, deletion-job and operational records or backup copies can remain after live account deletion for service protection, recovery or applicable legal obligations. They are not presented as active account data and are not included in the culinary export. Contact us for the retention and removal treatment applicable to your request. The public account deletion page lets you request deletion without reinstalling the app.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your account and cooking data and choose whether to use optional uploads, scanning, notifications and Nyam! product analytics.
  • Use the Android account-export screen to save the culinary ZIP, including supported account content and owned media. It excludes other members' private information, passwords, tokens, retired Health data, internal logs and backups. Very large exports or other platforms may require support.
  • Request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability from the contact below, including for older records absent from the app. Available rights and complaint routes depend on applicable law.

Nyam! is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. This statement does not mean that the app verifies a person's age. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us. The application does not offer medical or emergency support. Recipe, expiry and product information can be incomplete; inspect food and product labels and follow safe handling instructions.

8. Security, changes and contact

We use transport encryption, authorization, scoped access and upload controls. No service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in credentials and exported files private.

We update this policy when relevant practices change and revise the date above. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact the developer/operator, r.k.blank: nyamapp.contact@gmail.com.
